About Candy Con

Candy Con is a sparkly little universe of panels, posters, cosplay dreams, artist tables, random dance energy, midnight snack thoughts, and way too many fictional characters living rent-free in everyone’s head.

This page does not need to make perfect sense. It can be soft, chaotic, colorful, dramatic, cozy, loud, sleepy, magical, and slightly unserious all at the same time.

Come for the anime vibes, stay for the questionable playlist choices, shiny merch, dramatic stage lights, and the tiny joy of finding someone who likes the exact same obscure character as you.
